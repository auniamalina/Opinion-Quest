Grammar Helper: Indefinite Pronouns

Indefinite pronouns refer to people or things without saying exactly who or what they are!

Everyone / everyone All people in a group.
Someone An unspecified person.
Anyone Any person (used in questions & negatives).
No one Nobody / zero people.
Something An unspecified thing.
Anything Any thing (used in questions & negatives).
Nothing Not anything / zero things.
Everything All things in total.
